# Jetking Brand Guidelines

Use this for the website, print, social, decks, and centre materials so everything looks like one brand.

---

## Principles

1. **One accent** — Jetking red only. No second brand colour.
2. **Ink on paper** — dark text on white or light grey.
3. **Restraint** — clarity from type and space, not shadows or gradients.
4. **Readable red** — white text goes on `#C7141C`, never on `#EA1C24`.

## Typography

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Font** | Fira Sans |
| **Fallback** | Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif |
| **Weights** | 400 · 500 · 600 · 700 · 800 |

One typeface for headings and body. Do not mix extra display fonts.

| Role | Size | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Large title | 44–56px | Bold (700) |
| Section title | 32px | Bold |
| Card title | 24px | Bold |
| Body | 16px | Regular |
| Small / caption | 12–14px | Regular–Medium |

Titles: near-black `#101828` or brand red for short emphasis.  
Body: `#101828` or `#475467`.  
Long copy: keep line length comfortable (~70 characters).

---

## Layout habits

- Prefer white / `#F8F9FB` backgrounds.
- One red CTA per section.
- Thin grey dividers (`#E4E7EC`), not heavy coloured boxes.
- Real photos of Jetking centres, labs, and students.
- Soft corners on digital cards (~16px); avoid heavy shadows.

---

## Other channels

| Channel | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Social** | Light canvas, wordmark with clear space, dark headline, one red accent |
| **Print / boards** | White stock, logo + thin red rule + dark text; QR codes black on white |
| **Decks** | White title slide with wordmark; body in dark text; charts use neutrals + one red series |
| **Partners** | Do not recolour either logo; equal clear space |
| **Video** | Simple motion; red lower-thirds only if text stays readable |
