# Rationalization 03: Backend, Database, Features and Integrations

Scope: checklist sections 8 (backend), 9 (database and data management), 10 (features and integrations). Read-only audit of branch `backend-admin-cms`. Builds on `docs/ADMIN_AUDIT.md` (admin security findings are not repeated). Every claim below was checked with grep, ls or wc. Paths are relative to the repo root. Status: Done / Partial / Missing / N/A.

## Headline findings

1. **Supabase is dead weight.** The only live use of `@supabase/supabase-js` is a legacy dual-write in `src/lib/cms/store.ts` (lines 353, 388) that runs only when `DATABASE_URL` is unset but Supabase vars are set, plus a second sink in `src/features/jetking-ai/feedback-log.ts`. `supabase/migrations/20260331000000_init.sql` (228 lines: pgvector `documents`, `match_documents`, `staff_profiles`, `guide_transcripts`, `events`, plus `courses`/`cities`/`centres`...) has no reader: grep finds no use of `documents`, `match_documents`, `guide_transcripts`, `staff_profiles` or `events` anywhere in `src/` or `scripts/`. Retrieval runs from `src/content/jetking-embeddings.json`, not pgvector.
2. **Content has four storage paths** (details in section R1). The Postgres path is the real production path; the file store and Supabase are fallbacks that duplicate its logic.
3. **No retention policy for leads PII**, and the enquiry route logs the full payload (name, phone, email) to stdout on store or CRM failure.
4. **No email/SMS/notification service exists.** A new lead only reaches staff through the admin Leads screen or the optional `CRM_ENDPOINT`. No retry or queue when the CRM call fails.
5. **~25 MB of generated JSON is committed**; about 2 MB of it is unreferenced.

## Top 10 priorities

1. **Leads PII retention and log redaction** (section 9). Add a retention policy and purge script for `leads`, `chat_users`, `chat_conversations`, `admin_audit_log`; stop logging full enquiry payloads (`api/enquiry/route.ts`). Effort M, highest legal exposure.
2. **Remove Supabase** (R2). Dead dependency, conflicting SQL, two unanswered-question sinks. Effort S.
3. **New-lead notification and CRM retry** (section 8). No email/SMS/WhatsApp alert exists; a failed CRM post stays failed. Effort M.
4. **Backups and restore drill** documented for Postgres, which now holds leads, content and media bytes. Effort S.
5. **Collapse CMS storage layers behind one repository** (R1). Largest maintainability win; lean on the existing 113 CMS tests. Effort L.
6. **Delete dead data and reconcile env docs** (R6, R10, R8). Immediate cleanup, about 2 MB of repo weight and stale instructions. Effort S.
7. **Shared rate-limit store or explicit single-instance decision** (section 8). Redis branch is stubbed. Effort S/M.
8. **Add CI** (`npm run check`, plus a Postgres service for the four DB tests); no `.github/` exists. Effort S.
9. **Generated-artifact policy** for the 17 MB embeddings and 4 MB corpus (R4, R7). Effort M.
10. **DB hardening**: CHECK constraints from a single lead vocabulary (R5), extra lead indexes, TLS verification option for `rejectUnauthorized:false`. Effort S.
